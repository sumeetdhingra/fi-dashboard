Setup Instructions (click to expand) 
1. Create a new Google Sheet (any name).
2. In the sheet menu: Extensions → Apps Script.
3. Delete the default code, paste the contents of apps-script.gs (provided alongside this file).
4. Click Deploy → New deployment → Type: Web app.
   - Description: FI Dashboard
   - Execute as: Me
   - Who has access: Anyone
   Click Deploy and authorize the script.
5. Copy the Web App URL (ends with /exec) and paste it above.
6. Click "Save Connection" then "Test Connection".

The script will auto-create two tabs in your sheet: "Common" and "Investments".
Your data is stored in YOUR Google Sheet only — nothing is sent anywhere else.
